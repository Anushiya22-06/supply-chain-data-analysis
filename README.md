**Supply Chain Operations, Delivery Performance & Profitability Analytics**

An end-to-end data analytics project focused on analyzing supply chain operations, sales performance, profitability, delivery risks, customer behavior, and regional performance using Python, SQL, Excel, and Power BI.

## Project Overview

Supply chain businesses generate large amounts of data across orders, customers, products, sales, and deliveries. Analyzing this data helps businesses understand performance, identify operational issues, and make data-driven decisions.

**Project ALO** uses the DataCo Smart Supply Chain dataset to explore business performance, identify meaningful patterns, and present actionable insights through data analysis and interactive Power BI dashboards.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze sales and profitability across product categories.
- Evaluate shipping performance and late-delivery risks.
- Identify high-performing products and customer segments.
- Explore customer and regional sales performance.
- Develop interactive dashboards to communicate business insights.
- Provide data-driven recommendations to support business decisions.

## Dataset

**Dataset:** DataCo Smart Supply Chain Dataset

The dataset contains approximately 180,519 records covering orders, customers, products, sales, profitability, shipping, and delivery performance.

The dataset was used to investigate operational and business performance through data analytics.

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning — Python

- Inspected dataset structure, column types, and data quality.
- Checked for missing values and duplicate records.
- Converted order and shipping date columns to appropriate datetime formats.
- Removed selected unnecessary columns.
- Validated the cleaned dataset before analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis — Python

- Analyzed sales and profitability distributions.
- Compared product categories by sales, quantity, and profit.
- Examined shipping modes and delivery status.
- Compared actual and scheduled shipping durations.
- Explored patterns relevant to supply chain performance.
- Created visualizations to communicate analytical findings.

### 3. SQL Analysis — MySQL

- Used SQL queries to investigate supply chain data.
- Explored business metrics and operational performance.
- Analyzed data to support business-focused questions and reporting.

### 4. Interactive Dashboard Development — Power BI

Developed a Power BI report containing five dashboard pages:

1. **Executive Overview** — High-level business performance and key metrics.
2. **Sales & Profitability Analysis** — Sales and profit performance across products and categories.
3. **Delivery Performance Analysis** — Shipping modes, delivery status, and late-delivery risk.
4. **Product Performance Analysis** — Product and category performance across key metrics.
5. **Customer & Regional Analysis** — Customer segments, countries, and regional sales performance.

The dashboards provide an interactive way to explore business metrics and identify areas that may require further investigation.

## Key Business Insights

Initial exploratory analysis highlighted the following findings:

- **Product performance:** Fishing, Cleats, and Camping & Hiking were among the leading categories in sales and profit in the initial analysis.
- **Delivery performance:** Comparing actual and scheduled shipping durations helps investigate delivery delays and operational performance.
- **Delivery risk:** The dataset includes both high-risk and lower-risk delivery records, supporting further analysis of delivery outcomes.
- **Customer and regional performance:** Comparing customer segments and geographic areas helps identify where sales are concentrated.

These findings can help businesses investigate product performance, monitor delivery operations, and prioritize areas for improvement.

## Business Recommendations

- Monitor delivery performance across shipping modes to identify potential delay patterns.
- Review product-level sales and profitability to support inventory and product planning.
- Track regional and customer-segment performance to identify growth opportunities.
- Use interactive dashboards for regular performance monitoring and evidence-based decision-making.

Recommendations should be evaluated alongside business context and additional operational data before implementation.
